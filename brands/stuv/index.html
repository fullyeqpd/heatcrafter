COMMON QUESTIONS
Stûv questions, answered
Where are Stûv stoves made?
Stûv is designed and manufactured in Belgium, building minimalist wood stoves and inserts since the 1980s.
How much does a Stûv cost at HeatCrafter?
The Stûv6 wood insert is $12,500 and the Stûv16 wood stove is $14,500. With Chimney Monkey's wood-burning installation, most Stûv projects land between $15,000 and $21,000 all-in, depending on flue and finish.
Can I see a Stûv in Buffalo Grove?
Yes — Stûv stoves and inserts are on display in our Buffalo Grove showroom, by appointment. These are design objects; they're worth seeing in person.
Who installs and services Stûv on the North Shore?
Chimney Monkey — our partner install team — handles the wood-burning installation, flue, and clearances, plus annual service, from Evanston to Lake Forest.
What about warranty and registration?
We register every unit we sell, and Chimney Monkey services what we install.
