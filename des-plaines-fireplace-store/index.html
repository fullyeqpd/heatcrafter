DES PLAINES FAQ
Questions from Des Plaines homeowners
How do showroom visits work for Des Plaines homeowners?
You book a time and visit our Buffalo Grove display room — about 20 minutes from Des Plaines. We light the fireplaces you're weighing so you can compare flame, heat, and finish, then spec the right model for your home.
Do you handle permits in Des Plaines?
Yes. Most gas fireplace, insert, and log-set installs in Des Plaines need a city permit. Chimney Monkey pulls the Des Plaines permit and handles the inspection, so you don't have to.
Can you convert my old wood-burning firebox to gas?
Usually yes. We drop a sealed gas insert or a vented or ventless log set into an existing masonry firebox, and Chimney Monkey relines the flue where needed. HeatCrafter sells the unit; Chimney Monkey installs it.
What does a fireplace cost in Des Plaines?
It depends on the type. As a guide: gas log sets run $1,300–$3,500 installed, gas inserts $9,000–$14,000 all-in, direct-vent fireplaces $8,000–$16,000 with framing, electric $224–$3,000, and water-vapor units from $580. We give a firm quote after your visit.
Do you service what you sell?
Yes. Chimney Monkey services and maintains everything HeatCrafter sells, and handles chimney sweeping, relining, and repairs across Des Plaines.
