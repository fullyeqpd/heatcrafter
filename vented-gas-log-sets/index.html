Vented Gas Log Sets: Cost, Types, Brands & How to Choose

What is a vented gas log set?
A vented gas log set is a ceramic or refractory log arrangement over a gas burner, installed inside an existing wood-burning fireplace. It burns with the damper open, sending exhaust up the chimney — which is why the flames look tall, yellow, and convincingly real. You keep the fireplace you have; you just stop hauling wood and cleaning ash.
Vented logs are the right call when the fireplace is about atmosphere. They produce less usable heat than an insert (most of it goes up the flue), but no other gas option matches the look of a real wood fire.

Vented vs. ventless: which do you want?
VENTED
• Real-wood look, tall yellow flames
• Damper stays open; exhaust up the chimney
• Less heat into the room
• Needs a working chimney
• Best for: atmosphere & authenticity
VENTLESS
• Nearly 100% of heat stays in the room
• No chimney needed; burns very clean
• Smaller, blue-tinged flames
• Illinois allows with an ODS safety pilot
• Best for: maximum heat & flexibility
We stock both — and both are burning in the Buffalo Grove display room, so you can compare the flame side by side before deciding. Full comparison: Vented vs Ventless Gas Logs guide.
Types & sizes
Log sets are sized to your firebox — measure the front width, back width, and depth. As a rule of thumb: fireboxes 24"–28" wide take an 18" set, 28"–34" take a 24" set, and 34"+ can handle a 30" set. Chimney Monkey confirms fit during the site visit.

Installation & safety
Vented logs are not a DIY project in Illinois — they connect to gas and rely on a working chimney. Every HeatCrafter log set is installed by Chimney Monkey: chimney inspection first, then burner setup, leak test, damper clamp, and a walkthrough of the controls. Most installs take half a day.
Frequently asked questions
Can I leave the damper closed with vented logs?
No. Vented logs require the damper open (a damper clamp is installed for safety). If you want closed-damper heat, look at a ventless set or a gas insert instead.
Do vented gas logs heat a room?
Somewhat — expect ambiance more than heat. For real heating, a gas fireplace insert delivers 3–4× more warmth from the same firebox.
How long do gas log sets last?
Quality ceramic sets last 10–15+ years. Real Fyre burners carry a lifetime warranty; logs themselves can be refreshed or replaced individually.
Do I need my chimney inspected first?
Yes — code and common sense. The install includes a Chimney Monkey inspection; any needed repairs are quoted before work begins.
Can I convert to a gas insert later?
Yes. Many clients start with logs and upgrade to an insert for heat later — the gas line work carries over.
