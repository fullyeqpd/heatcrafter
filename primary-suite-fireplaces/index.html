COMMON QUESTIONS
Primary suite fireplace questions
Can I run it from bed?
Yes. Most linear gas and electric fireplaces come with a remote, and many add a wall thermostat or a phone app, so you can set the flame and temperature without getting up.
Is it safe to leave on overnight?
Electric and water vapor units are safe to run all night — no combustion. Gas fireplaces are best set on a timer or thermostat rather than left unattended while you sleep.
Linear or traditional — which suits a primary suite?
Linear reads modern and serene, spanning a wall like a piece of art; traditional feels classic and cozy. Both are lit side by side in our display room so you can see which fits your suite.
How much does a primary suite fireplace cost?
Linear direct vent gas fireplaces run $8,000–$16,000 installed with framing; electric units $224–$3,000; water vapor from $580. Every project gets a fixed quote after a free site visit.
Can I add one during a suite remodel?
That's the perfect time — running gas, venting, and framing is easiest while walls are open. Chimney Monkey coordinates with your remodel and handles permits, typically adding 2–5 days for the fireplace.
