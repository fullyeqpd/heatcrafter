3

5
Inspection & walkthrough

What a proper install includes
Gas line connection and a full leak test
Direct-vent or stainless liner installation
Framing, clearances, and non-combustible finish
Village permit and municipal inspection
Carbon-monoxide and clearance safety check
Haul-away of your old unit
Why it's worth doing right
A fireplace is a gas appliance. Improper venting or connections risk carbon monoxide and fire hazards — which is why every HeatCrafter install is done by licensed, insured Chimney Monkey technicians and passed by a municipal inspector.

INSTALLATION FAQ
Installation questions, answered
Can you install a fireplace I bought somewhere else?
Often, yes. Chimney Monkey can quote installation on many units after a quick safety check to confirm it's compatible and up to code.
Do you handle the permit and inspection?
Yes. Chimney Monkey pulls the village permit and coordinates the municipal inspection as part of the job.
How long does installation take?
Most gas inserts and fireplaces install in a single day. Running a new gas line or chimney liner can add time.
Do you service what you install?
Yes — Chimney Monkey provides ongoing cleaning, service, and repair, so you have one local team for the life of the fireplace.
