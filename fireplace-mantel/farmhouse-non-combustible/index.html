Overview
This Lexington Hearth mantel is a complete surround — mantel shelf, legs, and face — cast from a non-combustible composite that looks like reclaimed wood and hand-hewn timber but can't catch fire. That's what makes the farmhouse look work in a modern room.
Because there's no combustible clearance to worry about, you can bring trim tight to the firebox and mount a television directly above the mantel. It's custom-sized to your wall and finished to match your décor, and it pairs with any gas insert or direct vent fireplace in our showroom.

COMMON QUESTIONS
Mantel questions, answered
Why does a non-combustible mantel matter?
Fireplaces have required clearances to anything that can burn. A non-combustible mantel lets you bring trim tight to the firebox and mount a TV directly above without violating those clearances — the farmhouse look, done safely.
Can it fit my existing fireplace?
Yes — it's custom-sized. Bring your wall and opening dimensions to your showroom visit and we'll build the mantel and surround to fit your fireplace, whether it's existing or new.
Can I add it during a fireplace install?
Absolutely — that's the most popular combination. When Chimney Monkey installs your gas insert or direct vent fireplace, they set the mantel and surround at the same time, so it's finished in one visit.
What does it cost installed?
The mantel and surround start at $11,994. Installed cost depends on size, finish, and whether it's paired with a new fireplace; you get a fixed quote after we size it at your showroom visit.
What's the lead time?
Because it's custom-sized and finished, plan on a few weeks from approved sizing to install. We time it to land with your fireplace so everything is set in one visit.
